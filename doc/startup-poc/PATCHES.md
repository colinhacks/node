# Extended patch digest

The frozen [18-patch series](patches/series) preserves the original 15 diffs byte-for-byte and adds three changes. The [source commits](COMMITS.md) record the corresponding signed-off Git history. The tables explain every patch, its measured margin or lack of isolated attribution, and its compatibility costs.

The current branch has **22 source patches**. The [complete package](linux-renewed/final-v1/source-package/README.md) includes four renewed Linux changes, with final measurements and prerequisite checks; the original 18 files remain unchanged.

## Original 15 patches

These marginal measurements are historical macOS arm64 results, not Linux or Windows attribution. Ratios are paired control/candidate process-lifetime ratios with 95% bootstrap intervals; higher means faster. Experiments used different historical controls, and grouped results are labeled. Margins cannot be added or multiplied into the combined speedup.

## Renewed Linux additions

The three new V8 changes together improve empty startup by **5.4–5.8% in speedup ratio** over the frozen source fork in the final normal-build comparison. The complete source/compiler recipe reaches **1.52–1.55×** versus pristine normal; no individual margin below should be multiplied into that result.

| Addition | Change | Marginal evidence | Compatibility and maintenance |
